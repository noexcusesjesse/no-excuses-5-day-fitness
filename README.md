# No Excuses 5-Day Fitness Program

Free beginner-friendly community program featuring resistance tubes/bands, kettlebells, bodyweight training and walking.

## Weekly plan
- Monday: Full-body strength
- Tuesday: Upper body and core
- Wednesday: Lower body and balance
- Thursday: Active recovery
- Friday: Full-body strength
- Saturday: Bodyweight and conditioning
- Sunday: Three easy 20-minute walks

Workouts are 30 minutes: 5-minute warm-up, 20-minute strength circuit, 5-minute cooldown. Walking goal on workout days: 10,000 steps or 90 minutes, adjusted to ability.
